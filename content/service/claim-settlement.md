## What this service is
- A to Z assistance to file, track and close legitimate claims and recover investments.

- Form filling & submission assistance

- Periodic status updates until closure

- Higher probability of successful outcomes
- Less stress and faster turnaround
- Clarity on required proofs and forms
- Transparent progress tracking
